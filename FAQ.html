Frequently Asked Questions

What is MyTravaly?
MyTravaly is a unified global travel infrastructure platform that connects travelers, hospitality leaders, and creators through intelligent technology, seamless distribution, and automated financial systems.
How does the platform help hotel owners?
We provide hotel owners with an integrated Property Management System (PMS), global distribution access via our marketplace, and AI-driven pricing intelligence to optimize occupancy and maximize revenue.
What is the MT Gateway program?
MT Gateway is a monetization infrastructure designed for travel creators and agents. It provides access to global inventory with customizable markups and instant automated payouts.
Is MyTravaly available globally?
Yes, MyTravaly operates as a borderless infrastructure, supporting properties and partners in over 190 countries with multi-currency settlement and localized support.
